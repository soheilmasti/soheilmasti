### 🏛️ About Me & BIMCO

I am a **Senior Computational Architect & Lead BIM Coordinator** with over 15 years of international experience across Europe and the Middle East, based in **Barcelona, Spain** and **Tehran**. 

Founder and Technical Lead at **[BIMCO](https://bimco.es/)** — a specialized nearshore architectural BIM production practice delivering high-precision **Autodesk Revit (LOD 200–500)** models, multi-disciplinary clash resolution, and ISO 19650 compliant technical packages for architectural practices across the **UK, Ireland, and Continental Europe**.
